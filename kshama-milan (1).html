A first look at Intesa Sanpaolo

Why them. Intesa says its Academy4Future programme trains its 90,000+ people in AI, data, risk and digital skills, with about 20,000 colleagues joining each year. It also runs remote sessions such as Vivichat. That is a lot of learning and communication content to store, deliver and measure.
Why us. Bending Spoons now owns Brightcove and Vimeo, both built for enterprise video hosting and streaming.
My hypothesis. Start with training video for Academy4Future, then internal town halls, then external events.

Built from public information only. I don't know which video tools they use today, so call one is for finding out.

Who I'd call, and how I'd know it's working

Order of calls. First, whoever owns Academy4Future: their problem is delivery at scale and proving completion. Then internal communications. In parallel, IT security and procurement, because a bank's vendor review, including EU rules such as DORA, sets the timeline.
First 90 days. Named decision-makers mapped, discovery calls held, one Academy4Future programme scoped as a pilot, and the security review started. I'd track those before revenue.
The objection I expect. There have been reports of team cuts at Vimeo and Brightcove since the acquisitions, and a bank will ask about continuity. I'd answer with written support terms and a named roadmap contact, and be straight about what I can't promise.
Feedback loop. Whatever Intesa tells me goes back to the product team.

Why start here

Bending Spoons buys established products and runs them for the long term, and Brightcove and Vimeo put enterprise video in that portfolio. I'd like to be the person who takes that to a bank like Intesa, listens closely, and stays to own the result. Grazie for your time!
